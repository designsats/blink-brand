# Blink — Logo

## Assets

All in `assets/logo/`. Use these files. Never redraw, re-trace, re-download or
reconstruct the logo.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `blink-logo-horizontal.svg` | **Default.** Light backgrounds |
| `blink-logo-horizontal-on-dark.svg` | Dark backgrounds |
| `blink-logo-tagline.svg` | With tagline, light backgrounds |
| `blink-logo-tagline-on-dark.svg` | With tagline, dark backgrounds |

## Construction

The logo is a horizontal lockup: the circle mark, then the wordmark.

- The circle mark carries the 45° gradient (`#ffbe0b` → `#fb5607`).
- The wordmark is solid — Venta black `#000000` or white `#FFFFFF`. Never gradient.
- The wordmark is set in Ubuntu Bold Italic, already outlined in the artwork.

### Clear space

Define `x` as the height of the circle mark. Maintain clear space of at least `x` on
all four sides of the lockup. Nothing enters that zone — no text, no graphics, no
image edges, no other logos, no slide furniture.

With the tagline lockup, the space below the tagline is `70%` of `x`.

### Minimum size

- Horizontal lockup: **50px** wide on screen. Below this, use the circle mark alone.
- Circle mark: **32px**. A 32px asset exists as `Blink_app-icon-32px.png` in the
  original brand book if you need a raster version.

## Backgrounds

Permitted, in order of preference:

1. White — full color lockup
2. Off-white `#F2F2F4` — full color lockup
3. Venta black or `#1d1d1d` — `-on-dark` lockup
4. Lightning yellow `#ffbe0b` — black lockup
5. The Blink gradient — white lockup
6. Blink blue `#5D78DA` — white lockup
7. Light grey — black lockup
8. Photography — white mono lockup, placed over a calm area of the image

Never place the logo on a busy area of a photograph. If no calm area exists, put the
logo on a solid block adjacent to the image instead.

## Prohibited

From the brand book's Prohibited Usage page, plus current practice:

- **Never make different versions of the logo.** No vertical stack, no mark-above-word.
  Horizontal only.
- **Never change the proportions** of the mark relative to the wordmark.
- **Never use unspecified colors.** The wordmark is black or white. The mark's gradient
  is fixed.
- **Never gradient the wordmark.** Only the circle uses the gradient.
- **Never deform or add effects** — no stretch, skew, rotate, shadow, glow, outline,
  bevel, 3D.
- **Never add elements into the clear space**, including taglines you invented,
  product names, or "×" co-branding marks placed too close.
- **Never re-type the wordmark.** Ubuntu Bold Italic plus a bitcoin symbol is not the
  Blink logo.
- **Never use the circle mark as a logo in a layout.** It is an app icon, favicon or
  avatar. In a deck, a document or a web header, use the full lockup.

## Tagline

**The Everyday Bitcoin Wallet** — always rendered `THE EVERYDAY BITCOIN WALLET` in the
lockup, and always as part of the supplied tagline artwork. It is never reworded,
never abbreviated, and never re-set by hand.

A Spanish lockup exists in the brand book. Localized taglines require design sign-off —
do not translate it yourself.

"Blink makes using and learning Bitcoin simple" is **marketing copy**, not the tagline.
It may be reworded freely and must never appear in the logo lockup.
